FAQ

Good to know.

Can I use Clyento in the US today?

Not yet. Clyento is live in France, and the US version is in preparation. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as soon as it opens.

Who is it for?

Small businesses that have never had time to prospect: contractors, salons, restaurants, coaches, agencies and freelancers. You describe what you do, Clyento decides which businesses to target.

I mostly sell to consumers. Does it work for me?

Yes, differently. Clyento never provides lists of private individuals. It finds local businesses that can send you customers: a physical therapist for a fitness coach, a hotel for a restaurant, a wedding planner for a hair salon.

How much will it cost?

Plans will be monthly, with no commitment, and you'll be able to cancel anytime. Waitlist members get founder pricing, kept for as long as they stay subscribed.
